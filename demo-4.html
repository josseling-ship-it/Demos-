Dr. Chisholm’s career has been defined by a single pursuit: the natural facelift. After earning his first professional doctorate at Dalhousie University and his medical degree from Louisiana State University, he built a practice devoted to the face and has long served on the surgical staff of Eisenhower Medical Center.
