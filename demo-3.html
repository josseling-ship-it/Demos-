Never a Formula

No two faces age the same way, so no two facelifts should be the same. Dr. Chisholm studies the skin, the deeper structures, and the natural proportions of each face before recommending an approach, from subtle refinement to comprehensive deep-plane rejuvenation.

Deep-Plane Facelift

Rather than simply pulling the skin, the deep-plane technique repositions the deeper layers of the face to restore the cheeks, jawline, and neck with a natural, unoperated look.
